# Changelog

What's changed in BestShot, newest first, in plain English. The same text goes
in the GitHub release and the App Store's "What's New", and the newest entry
shows once on the device after an update.

## Unreleased — Multi Crop and Glue extras (20 November 2026)

### Multi Crop
- A strip under the image shows each photo as it will be saved: straightened,
  trimmed and turned, in the order it will be numbered. Tap one to leave it
  out (or press Space on the focused box); tap again to put it back. `v`
  hides or shows the strip.
- Boxes snap to the image's edges and to each other's edges and sizes while
  you drag, so a contact sheet's boxes come out the same size. Hold ⌥ on the
  Mac to place freely.
- Press `#`, then the boxes in the order you want them numbered. Esc stops
  early; the rest keep their order after the ones you picked.
- A magnifier appears beside a corner while you drag it.

### Glue
- Drag a seam to move it within the overlap.
- Hold `\` (or the before icon) to see the photos simply butted together,
  with none of your settings, then let go to see your version again.
- Each join can have its own fade: focus a photo and use "This join" (or
  ⌥[ and ⌥]); the link icon puts it back to the shared fade.

### Monitor Mode
- Glue and Multi Crop show on the monitor when one is open on the phone or
  iPad, with their keys in the hint bar, and the keyboard drives the editor.
  While renumbering on the monitor, press 1–9 for the boxes.

## Multi Crop and Glue (19 November 2026)

### Multi Crop (iPhone, iPad, Mac)
- Pull several photos out of one image: a scanned page of prints, a contact
  sheet, a screenshot. It finds the photos when it opens; drag to draw more,
  move, resize, straighten (the handle above a box, or `[` and `]`), or switch
  a box to four corners (`p`) for a print shot at an angle.
- Turn any box a quarter before saving: the rotate icons, or `r` and `⇧R`.
- **Use last crop** (`l`) puts the previous image's boxes on this one, so a
  run of same-layout scans takes one key each.
- Makes `Scan_004-1.jpg`, `-2`… beside the source in Folders (⌥⌘C on the Mac),
  or ready for Save to Photos and Share. Straight boxes are exact copies of
  the original pixels.

### Glue (iPhone, iPad, Mac)
- Glue 2 to 10 photos into one: side by side, stacked, in a grid, or placed
  freely ("jagged") on a background colour or a blurred fill made from the
  photos.
- A gap or an overlap between photos, with a straight edge or a soft fade at
  the seam. Slide a photo across the join to line up a horizon, or let
  **Line up** find the overlap for you.
- Zoom the canvas freely: pinch, ⌘+ and ⌘−, ⌘0 to fit.
- In Folders, ⌘-click photos in the grid and press ⌥⌘G; the new photo,
  `IMG_0012-glued.jpg`, appears beside the first one.

### Both
- New files are new: they carry the date, camera, location and keywords of
  their source, but no stars or colour label. Saved as JPEG unless you choose
  HEIC, PNG or TIFF in the ⋯ menu.
- Originals are never changed. Undo after making files puts them in the Trash.
